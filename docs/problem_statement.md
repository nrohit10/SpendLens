# Problem Statement

## Background

Raw bank and credit card statements provide transaction-level data but do not
offer clear visibility into spending behavior, patterns, or optimization
opportunities without manual effort.

## Objective

The objective of SpendLens is to transform raw financial transactions into
clear, explainable insights that help an individual understand where money is
spent, why spending changes over time, and where expenses can be optimized.

## Key Questions

- In which categories am I spending money?
- What portion of my spending is recurring?
- What are my spending patterns over time?
- What is my average spending monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and yearly?
- Why did my spending spike in a given period?
- How does spending differ across payment instruments?
- How much is allocated to investments?
- Where can spending potentially be reduced?

## Scope

- Personal bank and credit card transactions
- Expense categorization and aggregation
- Pattern, trend, and anomaly analysis
- Explainable insights (not financial advice)

## Out of Scope

- Budget planning or enforcement
- Investment performance tracking
- UI-heavy dashboards
